| 🧩 ساختار | مقدمه، ۲ یا ۳ نکته‌ی اصلی، نتیجه‌گیری |

## 🧱 قالب سه‌بخشی لکچر

```text
1. Introduction (30-45 seconds)
   - Greeting
   - Hook: a question, a fact, or a short story
   - Topic sentence: "Today I want to talk about ..."

2. Body (2-3 minutes)
   - Point 1 + example
   - Point 2 + example
   - (Optional) Point 3 + example

3. Conclusion (30 seconds)
   - Short summary
   - Final thought or question
   - "Thank you for listening. Any questions?"
```

## 💬 جمله‌های آماده برای هر بخش

### شروع
- `Good morning, everyone. Today I'm going to talk about ...`
- `Have you ever wondered why ...?`
- `Did you know that ...?`

### رفتن از یک نکته به نکته‌ی بعد
- `First of all, ...`
- `Another important point is ...`
- `On the other hand, ...`

### مثال زدن
- `For example, ...`
- `Let me give you an example.`

### جمع‌بندی
- `To sum up, ...`
- `In conclusion, I think ...`
- `Thank you for your attention. Do you have any questions?`

## 📄 یک نمونه‌ی کامل کوتاه

**Topic: Why Breakfast Is Important**

> Good morning, everyone. Do you eat breakfast every day? Today I want to talk about why breakfast is important.
>
> First of all, breakfast gives us energy. After a long night, our body needs food to start the day. For example, students who eat breakfast can focus better in class.
>
> Another important point is health. People who eat a healthy breakfast usually eat less junk food during the day.
>
> To sum up, breakfast gives us energy and helps us stay healthy. So, don't skip it! Thank you for listening. Any questions?

**ترجمه‌ی خلاصه:** در این لکچر کوتاه، گوینده با یک سؤال شروع می‌کند، دو دلیل (انرژی و سلامتی) را با مثال توضیح می‌دهد و با یک جمع‌بندی ساده و دعوت به پرسش تمام می‌کند.
